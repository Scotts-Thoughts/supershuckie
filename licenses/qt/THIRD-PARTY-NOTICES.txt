Third-party code bundled inside the Qt 6 libraries (qtbase) that are
statically linked into SuperShuckie
=====================================================================

Qt itself is used under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3 (see
../LGPL-3.0-only.txt and ../NOTICE.txt). The components below are shipped
*inside* the Qt source tree and are therefore compiled into the Qt Core,
Qt Gui, Qt Widgets, Qt Svg, Qt OpenGL libraries and the Windows platform
plugin that SuperShuckie links. This list is derived from the
qt_attribution.json manifests in qtbase 6.11.1, restricted to the parts
that apply to a Windows build. Third-party libraries that Qt links
*externally* (FreeType, HarfBuzz, libpng, zlib, libjpeg-turbo, libtiff,
libwebp, libb2, PCRE2, ...) are listed separately under ../../libraries/.
