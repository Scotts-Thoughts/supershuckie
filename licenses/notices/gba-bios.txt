Game Boy Advance BIOS (bootrom/agb/gba_bios.bin)
================================================

SuperShuckie embeds an open-source replacement for the Game Boy Advance
BIOS, not Nintendo's. The 16 KiB image is byte-for-byte identical
(MD5 1876f71b0d8c65eef3454547896ffb11) to the one distributed with the
ReGBA / TempGBA emulator, whose source is in the "bios" directory of
https://github.com/Nebuleon/ReGBA. It is the "Custom GBA BIOS replacement"
based on VisualBoyAdvance-M's high-level BIOS emulation, as compiled by
Normmatt.
